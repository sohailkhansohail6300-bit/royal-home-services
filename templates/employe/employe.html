About Royal Home Services

Royal Home Services is a home-services platform built around one simple idea: a clean, well-kept home should feel effortless for the people who live there. Our role is to help customers find and work with cleaning and home-service professionals who are reliable, careful, and respectful of the spaces they enter. We do not promise miracles; we do promise a practical, honest process for connecting households with people who take their work seriously.

For many households, keeping up with everyday cleaning is a genuine challenge. Work schedules, family commitments, health conditions, and the sheer pace of modern life make it difficult to stay on top of dusting, mopping, disinfecting, and organising. A reliable cleaning professional can restore order to a home in a way that feels both practical and genuinely restorative. Our platform exists to make that relationship easier to establish.

Our approach to cleaning services

Cleaning is skilled work, not simply a matter of moving a cloth across a surface. A capable cleaning professional understands that a kitchen needs different treatment than a bedroom, that bathroom surfaces require care with disinfectants, and that delicate materials — natural stone, certain fabrics, wooden finishes — should never be treated with harsh chemicals. Our work focuses on residential cleaning tasks that households commonly request: general home cleaning and tidying, dusting of surfaces and furniture, kitchen cleaning including counters, stovetops and sinks, bathroom cleaning including tiles, fixtures and mirrors, floor care including sweeping, vacuuming and mopping, and the general maintenance of clean and organised living spaces.

We describe these tasks accurately because trust is built on honest expectations. Cleaning tasks that fall outside a typical residential scope — specialist restoration work, hazardous-material removal, or intensive industrial cleaning — are not automatically part of our offer. When a customer’s requirements extend beyond our standard residential scope, we aim to be straightforward about it, rather than making promises we cannot keep.

Our commitment to professional service

Good cleaning is inseparable from good behaviour. A cleaning professional enters someone’s private home. That access requires punctuality — arriving when agreed, or communicating promptly if anything changes. It requires respectful communication — speaking politely to customers, listening carefully to instructions, and asking for clarification when something is unclear. It requires attention to detail — noticing what has been asked for, and completing it thoroughly. It requires following instructions — not improvising with unfamiliar products or moving personal items without permission. And it requires treating customers and their property responsibly — leaving the home as it was found, reporting damage honestly, and never taking anything that was not offered.

Why cleaning professionals matter

Reliable cleaning professionals help customers maintain comfortable, organised, and healthy-feeling spaces. For a family with young children, a regular clean means floors that are safe to play on. For an older person living independently, it means a home that remains manageable. For a busy professional, it means a weekend free of chores. Behind each of those outcomes is a person who showed up when they said they would, did the work they were asked to do, and treated the home with care. Honesty, consistency, responsibility, and practical cleaning skills are not decorative qualities — they are the foundation of the service.

Customer privacy and safety

Cleaning professionals routinely see and learn things that customers expect to remain private: home addresses, alarm codes, personal possessions, family photographs, and often glimpses of customers’ private lives. Protecting that information is not optional. Addresses, access instructions, photographs, and personal details must be handled responsibly and never shared, discussed publicly, or used for any purpose other than completing the agreed work. Cleaning products must be used according to their labels and the manufacturer’s safety instructions, particularly where children, pets, or people with sensitivities are present. Any hazards encountered, and any incidents or accidents, should be reported through the appropriate company channel as soon as reasonably possible.

Our service standards

We expect cleaning professionals who register with us to deliver service that meets a clear and reasonable standard: completing agreed tasks thoroughly, communicating honestly, respecting customers, and adhering to applicable company procedures. Service quality is not judged by appearances alone — it is judged by whether the customer’s instructions were followed, whether the home was left in a better state, and whether the professional behaved in a way that builds trust. Where a customer is unsatisfied, we aim to handle the matter fairly and promptly, and we expect the same honesty from the professional that we expect from the customer.

Building trust together

Trust in home services is built gradually, through transparent communication, accurate information, and reliable conduct. When a cleaning professional explains what they can and cannot do, arrives when they said they would, treats the property with care, and reports problems honestly, trust grows. When customers give clear instructions and treat the professional with respect, that trust strengthens. Royal Home Services aims to support that process by keeping our expectations clear and honest, and by making it straightforward for reliable professionals to express their interest in working with us.
